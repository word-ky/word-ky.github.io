## About Me

I am **Jiaqi Wu (吴佳奇)**, a **Postdoctoral Researcher and Assistant Researcher** in the Department of Automation at **Tsinghua University**, working with [Gao Huang](https://gaohuang-net.github.io/) in [LEAP Lab](https://www.leaplab.ai/members).

My research focuses on **multimodal perception and distributed edge intelligence**: how to make intelligent systems perceive reliably in challenging environments and operate efficiently with limited computing and communication resources. I study visual grounding with vision-language models, visual enhancement and object detection, and collaborative learning and inference across cloud, edge, and terminal devices. My application interests include underground mining, underwater sensing, and power infrastructure inspection.

I am the **principal investigator of a 2026 NSFC Young Scientists Fund (Category C) project** and a **2026 CPSF–CCTEG Joint Special Funding project**. My publication record includes **15 published or accepted papers**, with first-author work in IEEE Communications Surveys & Tutorials (COMST; accepted), IEEE Transactions on Mobile Computing, IEEE Transactions on Multimedia, and ICCV, and corresponding-author work at CVPR. My recent first-author COMST survey brings together the architectures, resource management, collaborative learning, communication, and security foundations of cloud–edge–terminal intelligence. I also serve as **Leader Chair of an ICASSP 2027 Special Session** on distributed multimodal signal understanding with foundation models.

I received my Ph.D. in Information and Communication Engineering from **China University of Mining and Technology (Beijing)**, supervised by [Zijian Tian](https://ai.cumtb.edu.cn/en/info/1115/1331.htm). During my doctoral training, I undertook CSC-supported joint research at the **University of British Columbia**, working with [Victor C. M. Leung](https://people.ece.ubc.ca/~vleung/) and [Zehua Wang](https://people.ece.ubc.ca/~zwang/).

## Research Interests

- **Vision-language models and grounding:** connecting language instructions with visual targets and spatial evidence; multimodal alignment and efficient model adaptation.
- **Perception in challenging environments:** image enhancement and object detection under low illumination and other visual degradations, with applications in industrial sensing.
- **Distributed edge intelligence:** federated learning, efficient inference, and cloud–edge–terminal collaboration under heterogeneous data, devices, and resource constraints.
